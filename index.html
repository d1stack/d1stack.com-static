AI era

AI era

AI is a powerful tool. Experience is what makes it useful.

Businesses should absolutely use AI. It can accelerate research, development, analysis, automation, documentation and everyday decision-making.

But AI should not replace experienced people responsible for understanding the business, validating the output and making the final decisions.

EXPERIENCED PROFESSIONALS PROVIDE
ContextJudgement ArchitectureValidation Accountability

AI can make experienced people dramatically more effective. It does not remove the need for experience.

The metaphor

Think of AI as a great horse.

A good horse gives you speed, strength and the ability to travel much further than you could on your own. But the horse does not decide where you need to go.

Without an experienced rider, all that power can take you in the wrong direction.

AI works the same way. It is enormously capable, but without experienced people providing direction, judgement and control, it can become inefficient, misleading or even dangerous.

The advantage is not AI alone. The advantage is experienced people using AI well.
